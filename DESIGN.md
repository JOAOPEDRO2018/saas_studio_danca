## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for an operational management platform dedicated to dance academies, performing arts schools, and movement studios. The brand identity fuses the poise, artistry, and fluid movement of dance with rigorous, dependable SaaS efficiency.

### Target Audience
Studio owners, artistic directors, administrative managers, and dance instructors who need immediate clarity on enrollments, schedules, tuition settlements, and student metrics without being overwhelmed by sterile enterprise software.

### Emotional Response
The interface evokes feelings of grace, premium professionalism, composure, and effortless order. The software feels like a natural extension of an upscale boutique studio: structured yet graceful, functional yet visually inspiring.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic
A synthesis of **Refined Contemporary SaaS** and **Soft Editorial Minimalism**. The visual language utilizes crisp, airy alabaster surfaces, micro-borders with delicate opacity, fluid typography, and precise magenta accents derived from the brand emblem. Data density is achieved through immaculate hierarchy rather than visual clutter.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate brand connection through rich balletic magentas balanced against pristine neutral surfaces and authoritative deep tones.

### Functional Roles

- **Primary (`#b8387c` / `#c74b8c`)**: The core brand signature. Used for key interactive triggers, active tab states, calendar highlight markers, and primary call-to-actions.
- **Secondary (`#fcebf2`)**: A soft, translucent ballet tint used for hovered list rows, secondary tag fills, badge backdrops, and active schedule cell accents.
- **Tertiary (`#3d1326`)**: An ultra-deep plum-charcoal tone used for dark administrative sidebars, high-contrast modal headers, and premium metric cards.
- **Neutral Surface & Canvas**:
  - Main App Background: `#faf8f9`
  - Elevated Container / Card Background: `#ffffff`
  - Border Subdued: `#f0e6eb`
  - Border Distinct: `#e3d3dc`
- **Typography Neutrals**:
  - High Emphasis: `#1e1b1d` (softened charcoal, softer than pure black to preserve refinement)
  - Medium Emphasis: `#625960` (muted slate mauve)
  - Low Emphasis: `#988d95` (subtle captions and metadata)

### Status Tokens (Financial & Class Attendance)
- **Em Dia (Settled / Regular)**: `#15803d` text on `#f0fdf4` surface with `#bbf7d0` border.
- **Pendente (Pending / Due Soon)**: `#b45309` text on `#fffbeb` surface with `#fde68a` border.
- **Atrasado com Multa (Overdue / Penalized)**: `#be123c` text on `#fff1f2` surface with `#fecdd3` border.

## Typography

The typography system relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles. Its geometric architecture combined with friendly, sculpted curves delivers an approachable yet polished feel. It maintains supreme legibilidade in tight financial tables, tuition breakdown matrices, and multi-track dance schedule calendars.

### Usage Rules
- **Display & Large Headlines**: Reserved for executive dashboards, monthly revenue overviews, and hero summaries.
- **Numbers & Financial Figures**: In tables and KPI summaries, numerals must render with tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure vertical alignment across currencies and dates.
- **Labels & Badges**: Set in `label-sm` or `label-md` with semi-bold to bold weights and slight letter-spacing expansions to maintain scannability across high-density grids.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a flexible, responsive 12-column grid system paired with an 8pt architectural rhythm (`0.25rem`, `0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2.25rem`).

### Responsive Adaptation
- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column layout with fixed administrative navigation (260px wide) and scrollable fluid work canvas. 2rem outer margins and 1.25rem gutters. Weekly and monthly schedule planners span 7 continuous columns without truncation.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column layout. Navigation collapses into an icon-rail (72px) or off-canvas drawer. Data grids support horizontal inertia scrolling with sticky student-name columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout. Margins compress to 1rem. Calendar views shift from comprehensive multi-room weekly boards to swipeable day-view cards and stacked agenda views.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses delicate tonal surfaces, translucent backdrops, and soft tinted ambient shadows to reflect the light, airy atmosphere of a dance studio. Heavy drop shadows and abrupt dark edges are strictly avoided.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Flat `#faf8f9` backdrop. No shadows.
- **Level 1 (Standard Card & Table Container)**: Pure white background (`#ffffff`), encased in a crisp border (`1px solid #f0e6eb`), complemented by an ambient tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(61, 19, 38, 0.04), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(61, 19, 38, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Class Slot / Hovered Row / Popovers)**: Elevated cards with `box-shadow: 0 10px 24px -6px rgba(184, 56, 124, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -3px rgba(61, 19, 38, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Sheet Panels)**: Centered overlays or slide-over edit drawers with `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(61, 19, 38, 0.16)`. Combined with a subtle backdrop filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(6px)` over `rgba(30, 27, 29, 0.4)`).

## Shapes

The design system incorporates balanced, gentle curves (`roundedness: 2`) to express organic movement and balletic poise.

### Geometry Guidelines
- **Input Fields, Dropdowns & Standard Buttons**: `0.5rem` (8px) radius, providing a refined, approachable boundary.
- **Cards & Data Tables Containers**: `1rem` (16px) radius, softly framing administrative modules.
- **Status Badges, Chips & Avatars**: Fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) to create clear visual contrast against rectangular tables and schedule blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Background in `#b8387c`, text in `#ffffff`, subtle hover transition shifting to `#a02f6a` with `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px rgba(184, 56, 124, 0.25)`.
- **Secondary Button**: Background `#fcebf2`, text `#b8387c`, border `1px solid rgba(184, 56, 124, 0.2)`. On hover, background deepens to `#f9d8e5`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary Button**: Transparent background, text `#625960`, minimal hover surface `#f5eff3`.

### Status Badges (Financial & Academic)
- Compact pill-shaped tokens with uppercase or title-case text in `label-sm`.
- **Em Dia**: Pale emerald fill (`#f0fdf4`), green text (`#15803d`), border `#bbf7d0`. Accompanied by a solid 6px dot indicator.
- **Pendente**: Warm amber tint (`#fffbeb`), text (`#b45309`), border `#fde68a`.
- **Atrasado com Multa**: Rose tint (`#fff1f2`), deep crimson text (`#be123c`), border `#fecdd3`. Includes bolded penalty fee subscript.

### Form Inputs & Controls
- **Input Fields & Selects**: Height 42px, radius 8px, border `1px solid #e3d3dc`, background `#ffffff`. Focused state reveals a crisp outline: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(184, 56, 124, 0.15)` with border color `#b8387c`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom controls styled in `#b8387c` when checked, with a crisp white tick mark and smooth scale transitions.

### Cards & Financial Tables
- **Metrics Cards (KPIs)**: Feature top micro-borders tinted in primary magenta or light slate, distinct numeral sizes (`headline-lg`), and muted descriptive subtext.
- **Tables (Tuition & Enrollments)**: Header row in light mauve tint (`#faf5f8`) with uppercase label-md tracking. Table rows feature 52px minimum height, border-bottom `1px solid #f0e6eb`, and soft hover highlighting in `#fdf8fa`.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Class Schedule Slot (Grade de Horários)**: Micro-cards within schedule cells displaying dance style (e.g., *Ballet Clássico*, *Jazz Contemporâneo*), instructor tag, studio room name, and enrolled/capacity ratios (e.g., `14/18 alunas`). Color-coded left border strip denoting student tier (Infantil, Juvenil, Adulto).
- **Student Profile Strip**: Compact horizontally stacked card displaying dancer name, matriculation code, active modality tags, and a quick-action link to billing statements.
